Sensor data matters most, yet few teams check it systematically.

Each station has its own climate and quirks. Without a baseline per hour and per sensor, a warm afternoon and a broken probe look the same. The monitor builds that baseline from readings that no model flagged.

Bad data is usually found after it has already been used. Two models agree before an incident is raised, so the inbox stays short and the urgent items stay visible.

Every incident comes with a likely cause and one action: clean the probe, check the logger, exclude a spike, or compare with a neighbouring station.
