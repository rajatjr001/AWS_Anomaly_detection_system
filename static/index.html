How it works

From sensor to status in three steps

Readings follow the same path whether they come from a station or a manual entry.

01

Collect

Stations send readings to the ingestion API, and each one is saved to the database.

02

Score

A trained model labels every reading as normal, suspicious, or anomalous.

03

Review

The dashboard shows totals, a temperature trend, and the latest observations.
